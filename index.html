Introducing Skyler Dungan

My name is Skyler Dungan and I am a first semester journalism MA student at CUNY! I am originally from Texas, but I have been living in New York for the last 5 years. I am interested in writing stories related at women's health and the changing climate.

My Favorite Stories:

  1. Girlhood Exists in Palestine. You Just Have to Dig Under the Rubble to Find it by Palestinian writer @adaughterofpearl (aka haya) on Substack is a beautiful essay about grief and the complexities of growing up under occupation.
  2. The Toxic Logic Behind the Shaming of Cornell's Jane Doe by Anahid Nersessian is an interesting article that dissects the failures of modern feminists who are not as "sex positive" as they think they are.
  3. The Babies Left Behind in America by Caitlin Dickerson is a deeply detailed story about children who are seperated from their deported partents under the Trump administration.
